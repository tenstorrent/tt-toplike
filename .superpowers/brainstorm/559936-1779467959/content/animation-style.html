DRAM rows breathe

DRAM rows (▪) pulse brighter when bandwidth is high. Tensix cells show ░▒▓ fill by power. Border color = ASIC temp (teal→gold→red). No moving particles.

Pros

  • Low complexity
  • No flicker
  • Readable at a glance

Cons

  • No sense of direction
  • Reads vs writes indistinguishable

● teal = DRAM→Tensix read   ● pink = Tensix→DRAM write

Moving particles (reads + writes)

Teal particles (∘ ○ ·) drift from DRAM rows inward. Pink particles drift outward toward DRAM. Particle density = estimated bandwidth from power_w. Border = ASIC temp.

Pros

  • Conveys direction of data flow
  • Reads vs writes visually distinct
  • Matches tensix-viz feel

Cons

  • More screen noise
  • Bandwidth is inferred (power proxy)

Full: particles + L1 fill bars

Adds per-Tensix-cell fill bars (▁▂▃▄▅) showing relative L1 cache activity, alongside particles. Border color = temp. Closest to the original tensix-viz model.

Pros

  • Most information-dense
  • Per-cell L1 activity visible
  • Faithful to tensix-viz

Cons

  • Most complex to implement
  • tt-smi doesn't expose per-core L1 — must proxy from power distribution
  • Risk of visual clutter
