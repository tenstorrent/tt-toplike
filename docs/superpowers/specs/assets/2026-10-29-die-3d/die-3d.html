Top: what the terminal UI could really do. Each character cell is a half-block (▀) with a foreground and a background colour, which gives 2 pixels per cell in truecolour. Bottom: the egui version with real perspective. Drag it to rotate. Layout and heat are illustrative.

Terminal (ratatui): isometric board + die, 150 × 42 cells, truecolour half-blocks
egui window: real 3D. Drag to rotate.

What moves in both views

  • Tile height and colour: per-tile traffic. Real only with per-tile NoC counters. Until then it follows chip-wide load.
  • Blue stream from the PCIe edge: host traffic. Real now, once the counter fix lands.
  • Green blocks and streams at the sides: GDDR6 activity. Real now from the smbus telemetry.
  • Purple and cyan wires: NoC0 and NoC1. The two directions are real counters on a real card.
